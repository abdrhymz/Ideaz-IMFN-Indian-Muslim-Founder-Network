## 1. Bismillah — The Vision

**Indian Muslim Founders (IMF)** is an open-source, community-driven platform built *by* Indian Muslim entrepreneurs, *for* Indian Muslim entrepreneurs.

We are builders, dreamers, and problem-solvers from every corner of India — from the tech corridors of Bengaluru and Hyderabad to the trading hubs of Mumbai, the startup lanes of Delhi NCR, and the emerging ecosystems of Lucknow, Bhopal, and beyond.

This repository is not just code. It is a **sadaqah jariyah** — an ongoing act of collective good that outlives any single contributor. Every line committed, every issue raised, every mentorship offered is a seed planted for the generations of founders who will come after us.

We build in the open because the Prophet ﷺ said:

> *"None of you truly believes until he wishes for his brother what he wishes for himself."*
> — Sahih al-Bukhari

## 2. Why This Exists — The Need

Indian Muslim founders navigate a unique intersection of challenges:

- **Access gaps** in funding networks, incubators, and mentorship circles
- **Representation deficits** in mainstream startup media and ecosystem narratives
- **Community fragmentation** — brilliant founders scattered across cities, sectors, and languages with no unified gathering space
- **Cultural and religious context** that mainstream platforms rarely understand (halal fintech, modest fashion, Islamic education tech, waqf-based models, Ramadan-season commerce, etc.)

We are not building a silo. We are building a **home base** — a place of *sukoon* (tranquility) and *quwwah* (strength) — from which our founders engage the wider Indian and global startup ecosystem with confidence and clarity.

## 3. Our Goals

### 3.1 Community & Brotherhood/Sisterhood (*Ukhuwwah*)
- Create a verified, welcoming directory of Indian Muslim founders across all stages (idea → seed → scale → exit).
- Foster peer accountability circles (*halaqas* for founders) — small groups that meet regularly to share progress, struggles, and duas.

### 3.2 Knowledge Sharing (*Ilm*)
- Open-source playbooks: fundraising in India, GST/compliance, hiring, go-to-market for Bharat, bootstrapping guides.
- AMAs, founder stories, and post-mortems published freely under open licenses.
- Multilingual content — Hindi, Urdu, Tamil, Bengali, Malayalam, English, and more.

### 3.3 Access & Opportunity (*Rizq*)
- A curated, community-maintained list of Muslim-friendly and Muslim-founded VCs, angels, accelerators, and grant programs.
- Job/internship boards for Muslim-founded startups seeking talent.
- Partnership and co-founder matching.

### 3.4 Representation & Narrative
- Amplify success stories so the next 16-year-old in Malegaon or Moradabad *sees* themselves as a founder.
- Annual "State of Indian Muslim Founders" open-data report.

### 3.5 Ethical & Faith-Aligned Building
- Resources on Islamic finance, halal compliance, ethical AI, and building businesses that honour *amanah* (trust) and avoid *riba* (exploitation).
- A space to discuss the *fiqh* of modern business without judgment.

## 4. The Open Source Ethos — Shura in Code

This project is governed by the principle of **Shura** (mutual consultation):

> *"…and whose affair is [determined by] consultation among themselves…"*
> — Surah Ash-Shura (42:38)

- **The codebase is public.** Anyone can fork, propose, critique, and improve.
- **Decisions are made in the open** via GitHub Issues, Discussions, and community calls.
- **No single gatekeeper.** Maintainers are servants (*khuddam*) of the community, not owners.
- **All contributions are credited.** Every contributor's name goes in the `CONTRIBUTORS.md` — your *sadaqah* is recorded.

### Tech Stack (proposed — open to Shura)
| Layer | Tooling |
|-------|---------|
| Website / Community Portal | Next.js / Astro + Tailwind |
| Directory & Profiles | Headless CMS (Sanity / Strapi) |
| Discussions | GitHub Discussions + Discord |
| Data & Reports | Open datasets on `/data` |
| Docs & Playbooks | Markdown in `/docs`, rendered via Docusaurus |
| CI/CD | GitHub Actions |

## 5. How You Can Participate — Join the Jama'ah

You do **not** need to be a developer. You do **not** need to be a "successful" founder. If you are an Indian Muslim with a dream, a skill, or even just dua to offer — you belong here.

### 5.1 For Founders & Aspiring Founders
- ⭐ Star this repo and share it in your networks.
- 📝 Add your profile to the founder directory (PR welcome, or fill the form).
- 🗣️ Share your story, your failures, your lessons in `/docs/stories/`.
- 🤝 Offer or seek mentorship via GitHub Discussions.

### 5.2 For Developers & Designers
- 🛠️ Pick up `good-first-issue` and `help-wanted` labels.
- 🎨 Contribute UI/UX for the community portal.
- 📊 Help build the open-data reports and visualisations.
- 🔒 Review PRs, improve accessibility, write tests.

### 5.3 For Writers, Researchers & Translators
- ✍️ Draft playbooks, translate content into regional languages.
- 📚 Curate resources on Islamic business ethics, Indian startup policy, etc.

### 5.4 For Investors, Mentors & Ecosystem Builders
- 💡 List your fund/program in the community resource directory.
- 🎤 Offer office hours, AMAs, or workshop sessions.
- 📢 Amplify the project to your networks.

### 5.5 For Everyone
- 🤲 Make dua for this effort and for every founder struggling in silence.
- 🐛 Report bugs, typos, broken links — no contribution is too small.
- 💬 Be kind in Issues and Discussions. Adab (courtesy) is non-negotiable.

## 6. Contributing Guidelines

1. Read `CONTRIBUTING.md` before your first PR.
2. Follow the [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) spec.
3. Open an Issue before large PRs so we can discuss (*shura*) the approach.
4. All content is licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (docs) and **MIT** (code) unless stated otherwise.
5. Be patient, be generous in your reviews, and assume good faith (*husn al-zann*).

---

## 7. Code of Conduct — Adab al-Majlis

Our community adab is simple and non-negotiable:

- **Respect** every member regardless of madhab, sect, gender, caste, city, or stage of journey.
- **No gatekeeping.** Knowledge is a trust (*amanah*) from Allah; hoarding it is a betrayal.
- **No harassment, bigotry, or exclusion.** Violators will be removed.
- **Disagree with grace.** Critique ideas, never persons.
- **Protect privacy.** Do not share anyone's business metrics, personal struggles, or contact info without explicit consent.

Full details in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## 9. Governance

- **Maintainers** (initially 3–5 volunteers) handle repo hygiene, PR reviews, and community moderation.
- **Community Calls** (monthly, open to all) for major decisions — recorded and minuted in `/governance/`.
- **Annual Shura:** A broader review where any community member can propose direction changes.
- No funding is accepted that compromises editorial independence or community values.

## 10. A Final Word — Niyyah

This project is, at its core, an act of **niyyah** (intention).

We intend to build something useful. Something honest. Something that makes the path a little easier for the next Muslim kid in India who wants to build, create, and provide — without having to choose between their faith and their ambition.

We intend to do it together, in the open, with transparency and trust.

And we intend, if Allah wills, for every person who benefits from this work to be a source of ongoing reward for every contributor — a *sadaqah jariyah* that keeps flowing long after we have moved on.

> *"When a person dies, their deeds come to an end except for three: ongoing charity, knowledge that benefits others, and a righteous child who prays for them."*
> — Sahih Muslim

So build with us. Contribute. Share. Make dua.

**Bismillah. Let's begin.**
